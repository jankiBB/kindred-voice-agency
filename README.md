# Social Growth Hub

The attached PDF is a broader campaign strategy deck. It includes Google Ads, Meta Ads, bidding strategies, creative requirements, landing page references, social proof, case studies, and launch checklists. Use it as business and brand context, but do not reproduce the campaign architecture or internal advertising strategy on the website.

We need to create a premium Social Media Marketing landing page for Bombay Blokes.

Important: This is NOT a Paid Social / Meta Ads landing page and should NOT be positioned as a Performance Marketing landing page. The focus is organic/social media marketing: strategy, content, creative direction, social presence, brand storytelling, consistency, engagement, and overall social media growth. Paid social can be mentioned only where relevant as a supporting capability.

A user landing on the page should understand within 10–15 seconds:

“Can these people help my brand build a stronger and more effective social media presence?”

The page should be conversion-focused, but don't follow a predefined section structure. Think through the ideal user journey yourself and create the sections that make the most sense for a Social Media Marketing agency. Prioritize clarity, credibility, visual storytelling, social proof, actual work, case studies and conversion.

Do not copy the reference layout or content. Create a unique experience specifically for Bombay Blokes.

Design

I want a modern light-theme UI with:

Strong typography

Premium editorial feel

Generous whitespace

Clean layouts

Yellow accent highlights

High-quality imagery

Social media creatives

Smooth animations

Micro-interactions

Strong visual hierarchy

Use these colours as a starting point, but feel free to introduce supporting colours where needed:

#1D1D1D
#898989
#FAB31E
#ABABAB

Important UX Requirements

Lead-generation form prominently in the hero section

Keep the initial form simple; do not use a multi-step form

Strong visual storytelling

Include real/placeholder social media creatives rather than relying on text

Include meaningful case studies

Use interactive patterns where they genuinely improve the experience, such as stack cards, carousels, horizontal scrolling, hover interactions and scroll-based reveals

Make the page fully responsive and especially polished on mobile

Use the attached keyword research separately for the content/SEO direction. Naturally incorporate relevant keywords without keyword stuffing.

The final result should feel like a high-end Social Media Marketing agency website, not an SEO landing page, generic digital marketing website, or paid advertising landing page.

Think independently about the best information architecture, content hierarchy, interactions and section flow. Build the experience around what would most effectively convince a potential client to choose Bombay Blokes.
